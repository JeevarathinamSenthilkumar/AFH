The complete addendum is in `AFH-Addendum-MOSAIC-2025.pdf` and `data/faa-updates.json`. In-app FAA Updates presents its original text and page images as a separate layer. Original Chapter 17 remains intact for source fidelity and is explicitly flagged.

**Date discrepancy:** the addendum refers to July 18 and October 16, 2025. The current [FAA MOSAIC page](https://www.faa.gov/aircraft/MOSAIC) states publication July 24, 2025, pilot/repairman/maintenance changes effective October 22, 2025, and certification changes effective July 24, 2026. These dates are not silently corrected in the preserved document. Do not treat the older handbook as a current regulatory reference.
