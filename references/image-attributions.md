# Image attributions and public-release limitations

All handbook artwork is reproduced from the supplied 2021 FAA-H-8083-3C PDF for the requested local study application. Captions, source page images, and original acknowledgments are retained. The app icon is original vector geometry authored for this project; no FAA seal is used as the app identity.

The original acknowledgments (printed page iii, physical PDF page 6) credit:

- Shane Torgerson: Sedona Airport imagery, Chapter 1.
- Robert Frola: Evektor-Aerotechnik EV-97 SportStar Max imagery, identified as Chapter 16 in the source. The chapter reference may be an original numbering error and needs verification against the image itself.

**These credits do not state a downstream redistribution license.** No assertion is made that all handbook images are public domain. Figure 1-11 (Sedona Airport) and the SportStar Max imagery require an asset-specific license/permission review; other artwork also needs a rights audit before public distribution. Cropping or rendering a PDF page does not remove underlying third-party rights.

The hero is a crop from Figure 1-2's cockpit photograph, physical PDF page 23, used locally as original-source imagery. Its downstream rights have not been independently established.

Do not publish this complete asset collection to a public GitHub repository until the third-party material is cleared. All original material is retained locally; no unlicensed replacement imagery or generated educational diagrams have been substituted. The FAA catalog states that reproductions and modifications are the publisher's responsibility. Preserve these credits in any authorized release.

Sources: [FAA front matter](https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/airplane_handbook/01_afh_front.pdf), [FAA catalog](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation), [US government copyright guidance](https://www.usa.gov/government-copyright).
