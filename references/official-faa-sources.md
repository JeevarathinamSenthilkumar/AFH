# Official FAA sources

Verified November 2, 2026.

- [Airplane Flying Handbook landing page](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/airplane_handbook): FAA-H-8083-3C, 2021; all 18 chapters, front matter, glossary, and index.
- [Live aviation publications catalog](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation): lists 2021 edition and October 2025 addendum. No newer replacement was found in the live catalog or targeted FAA searches. This is a dated observation, not a claim about unpublished revisions.
- [October 20, 2025 MOSAIC addendum](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/AFH_Addendum_%28MOSAIC%29.pdf): preserved separately in full, including original page images and PDF.
- [Current FAA MOSAIC information](https://www.faa.gov/aircraft/MOSAIC): current official dates differ from dates mentioned in the addendum. The source is preserved as issued; consult this FAA page for rule information.
- [Official front matter and image acknowledgments](https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/airplane_handbook/01_afh_front.pdf).

The supplied local PDF is the authority for the Original Handbook Content layer. No original source passages are silently replaced by newer wording. Source references use the original printed page and physical PDF page. FAA source material is distinct from app interface text and supplemental practice questions.
